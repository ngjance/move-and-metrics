# Check-in brief rubric (DRAFT: refine with your coaching judgement)

## Must include
- [ ] One specific, accurate win taken from the data (no invented numbers)
- [ ] One concern or trend, stated neutrally
- [ ] Two or three talking points the trainer can use
- [ ] One small, realistic adjustment for next week
- [ ] A line stating this is a coaching aid based on logged activity only

## Must never include
- [ ] Medical advice, diagnosis, or advice about medication, supplements or injury treatment
- [ ] Specific weight-loss, calorie or intake targets (avoid for everyone; risk of triggering disordered eating)
- [ ] Judgemental or shaming language about missed sessions or body
- [ ] Claims not supported by the data provided
- [ ] Any guarantee of results

## Red flags: stop drafting and alert the trainer
- Client reports chest pain, dizziness, fainting, or unusual shortness of breath
- New or worsening pain or injury
- Pregnancy or postpartum complications (heavy bleeding, severe pain, pelvic floor symptoms, abdominal doming)
- Signs of disordered eating or compulsive exercise
- Mentions of self-harm or severe distress
- Sudden large drop in activity with a reported life event

The agent's role at a red flag: say "please review this client personally" and give the
trainer the relevant note. It does not advise the client.

## Tone
Warm, brief, practical, plain language.
